## 5. Your rights

You can exercise the following rights under PDPA (TH), PDP Law (ID), PDPD (VN), PIPL (CN) and similar laws:
- **Access / portability:** `Settings → Account → Export my data` downloads all personal data we hold (JSON).
- **Deletion:** `Settings → Account → Delete account`. You type a confirmation word to proceed. Deletion is scheduled for **30 days** later; logging in during that time lets you cancel. After 30 days, personal data is permanently erased and purchase records are anonymized.
- **Correction:** change your display name and linked email in Settings.
- **Withdraw consent:** turn off analytics in Settings at any time.
- **Complain:** contact us at [[privacy@…]] or your local data-protection authority.

We respond within [[30]] days, or within the time limit set by your local law.

## 6. Children

The game is not directed at children under [[13 / the local age of digital consent]]. Where local law requires it (e.g. China's minor-protection rules), we apply age verification and playtime limits.

## 7. Security

Measures include:
- TLS encryption in transit,
- passwords hashed with Argon2,
- refresh tokens stored hashed,
- row-locked transactions and an audit trail for all currency changes,
- access to production data limited to authorized staff.

## 8. Changes

When this policy changes materially, the game asks for your consent again before you continue playing (version-checked).
